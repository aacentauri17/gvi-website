The Problem

Most channel incentive programs fail quietly.

Dealers and distributors receive rewards that arrive late, feel generic, or never arrive at all. The result is a program that costs money but moves no needles — no increase in sell-through, no loyalty, no data.

The problem is rarely the budget. It is almost always execution: sourcing the right reward catalog, managing fulfilment at scale, building the digital infrastructure, and closing the loop with analytics.

Most companies lack the in-house machinery to do all four simultaneously. GroRewardz is that machinery.

How It Works

From brief to fulfilment in four steps.

We have run this process for insurance companies, cement manufacturers, automotive distributors and NBFCs. The program is tailored entirely to your channel structure.

01

Program Design

A complimentary 2-hour discovery workshop. Your channel structure, sales targets and reward preferences shape every element of the program.

02

Platform Setup

We configure the platform with your branding, participant roster, reward catalog and campaign rules. KYC, SSO and performance tiers — handled entirely by us.

03

Ongoing Management

Points allocation, reward sourcing, vendor coordination, helpdesk support and communications. We run the program so your team doesn't have to.

04

Analytics & MIS

Real-time dashboards for admin teams and regional managers. Redemption data, points flow, participant engagement — all in one place.
