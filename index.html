Most channel incentive programs fail quietly.

Dealers and distributors receive rewards that arrive late, feel generic, or never arrive at all. Sell-through stays flat, the channel stops paying attention, and nobody can tell you which campaigns worked.

What breaks is execution. Four things have to work at once: sourcing a catalog dealers actually want, fulfilling at scale, running the digital infrastructure, and reporting on what moved.

Most companies do not have the in-house machinery to run all four at once. GroRewardz is that machinery.

From brief to fulfilment in four steps.

We have run this process for insurance companies, cement manufacturers, automotive distributors and NBFCs. The program is built around your channel structure.

01

Program Design

A complimentary two-hour discovery workshop. Your channel structure, sales targets and reward preferences shape every element of the program.

02

Platform Setup

We configure the platform with your branding, participant roster, reward catalog and campaign rules. We handle KYC, SSO and performance tiers.

03

Ongoing Management

Points allocation, reward sourcing, vendor coordination, helpdesk support and participant communications. We run the program so your team does not have to.

04

Analytics & MIS

Real-time dashboards for admin teams and regional managers, covering redemption data, points flow and participant engagement.

A Mumbai business since 1943.

GroRewardz is the loyalty and rewards platform of GroValue Industries Pvt Ltd, part of the Aranha Agencies Group, a Mumbai business established in 1943. GroValue Industries was registered as a private limited company in 2013 and works out of Mahim, Mumbai.

Rahul Aranha, MD and CEO, has spent 11 years advising India's top corporates on loyalty program design, after 16 years at Honeywell, Booz Allen Hamilton, Citigroup and TCS. He ran EMEA as General Manager for Honeywell Aerospace Defense with direct P&L ownership of $300M.
