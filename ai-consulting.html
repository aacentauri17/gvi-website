Most AI pilots never reach production.

A team builds a working demo, it impresses in a meeting, and then it stalls. No owner, no integration path into the systems people actually use, no measurement of what it is worth once it is live.

The gap is rarely the model. It is scoping the right problem, integrating with live data and existing workflows, and having someone accountable for the outcome after launch — not just the prototype.

We treat AI projects the way we have run loyalty programs for 13+ years: as something we own end to end, not something we hand off half-built.

“In every AI engagement we have picked up after a stalled pilot, the pattern was the same: a proof of concept that worked in isolation, and no plan for what happens when it meets a live system.”
